## Professional & Fieldwork Engagements

**Assistant Urban Planner — Sheltech Consultants (Pvt.) Ltd.**
* **Project:** Improving Urban Governance & Infrastructure Project (IUGIP) - Package 04
* **Duration:** January 1, 2025 – May, 2025
* **Location:** Head Office (Dhaka) with frequent site visits
* **Role & Tasks:** Master plan preparation for 16 municipalities (Pourashavas) in Chattogram Division (LGD/LGED). Digitizing drone imagery, updating physical features and road networks for Raypur Pourashava, conducting QC checks, and maintaining geospatial attribute tables.

## Academic & Departmental Highlights

**Award Ceremony — Planning Carnival 2022**
* **Event:** World Town Planning Day 2022 Celebration
* **Organizers:** Dept. of URP, RUET & Bangladesh Institute of Planners (BIP)
* **Achievement:** **Champions** in Poster Presentation
* **Summary:** Participatory Rapid Appraisal (PRA) conducted across three communities in Rajshahi City revealed inadequate healthcare and physical training infrastructure. Proposed an integrated Physical Health Training Center (PHT) to address holistic community development.
