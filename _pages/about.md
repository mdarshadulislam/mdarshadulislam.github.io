## Overview

I am Md. Arshadul Islam, a [Lecturer](https://ruet.ac.bd/arshad) in the [Department of Urban & Regional Planning](https://www.urp.ruet.ac.bd/) at [Rajshahi University of Engineering & Technology (RUET)](https://ruet.ac.bd/). I completed my Bachelor of Urban & Regional Planning (BURP) from RUET in 2024, securing **1st position** in my cohort, and am currently pursuing my Master of Urban & Regional Planning (MURP) at the same institution.

My academic and research interests lie at the intersection of urban planning, geospatial methods, and computational approaches. I am particularly interested in using GIS, remote sensing, and emerging data-driven methods to understand urban change, spatial inequalities, and planning challenges, with an emphasis on evidence-based spatial planning.

Before joining academia, I worked as an Assistant Urban Planner at [Sheltech Consultants (Pvt.) Ltd.](https://www.scplbd.com), where I contributed to the preparation of master plans for 16 municipalities under the government's Improving Urban Governance and Infrastructure Project (IUGIP).

---

## Research Interests

* **Geospatial Analytics & Remote Sensing:** GIS, Google Earth Engine (GEE), satellite image classification, and spatial statistics.
* **Spatial Equity & Accessibility:** Urban green spaces, public amenity distribution, and environmental justice.
* **Urban Climate Resilience:** Climate-related urban hazards, urban heat dynamics, and adaptation strategies.
* **Inclusive & Participatory Planning:** Community-led planning, participatory GIS (PGIS), and resident-tourism dynamics.
