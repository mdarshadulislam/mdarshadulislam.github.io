I teach undergraduate theory and studio/sessional courses in the **Department of Urban & Regional Planning (URP)** and the **Department of Architecture** at Rajshahi University of Engineering & Technology (RUET). My instruction focuses on statistical methods, physical planning history, local governance systems, cartography, and practical urban planning studios.

---

## Theory Courses

### URP 2141: Statistics for Planners I
* **Department:** Urban & Regional Planning, RUET

**Topics Taught:**
* Descriptive statistics and presentation of planning data
* Measures of central tendency (arithmetic mean, geometric mean, weighted mean, median, mode)
* Measures of dispersion (variance, standard deviation, coefficient of variation)
* Skewness and kurtosis
* Empirical and Chebyshev's theorems
* Index numbers: types, uses, weighted/unweighted indices, Laspeyres and Paasche methods

---

### URP 2151: Physical Planning I
* **Department:** Architecture, RUET

**Topics Taught:**
* Determinants of the form, nature, and character of cities
* Origin and evolution of human settlements and cities
* City planning across historic periods (Ancient, Classical, Medieval, Neo-Classical, Renaissance, Baroque)
* Modern city planning, the Industrial Revolution, and urban development
* Utopian planning concepts (Garden City, Vertical City, Linear City)
* Neighbourhood planning and Radburn layout
* Spatial patterns and structures of urban growth

---

### HUM 3203: Political Science & Local Government
* **Department:** Urban & Regional Planning, RUET
* **Module Responsibility:** Local Government Portion

**Topics Taught:**
* Local government system in Bangladesh and its evolution
* Local government and planning in urban and rural contexts
* Resource mobilization at the local level and local-national government linkages
* Information flows, local planning frameworks, and national policy integration
* Community governance, partnerships, and public participation (focusing on gender inclusion)
* Sustainability, resilience, and capacity building of local governing bodies
* Stakeholder involvement, governance resources, challenges, and policy options

## Sessional & Studio Courses

### URP 1222: Surveying and Cartography
* **Department:** Urban & Regional Planning, RUET

**Topics Taught:**
* Map elements: legends, symbology, scale types, representative fraction (RF), and north arrows
* Map drawing, enlargement, reduction, and manual area calculations
* Practical choropleth mapping techniques

---

### URP 3102: Urban Planning Studio
* **Department:** Urban & Regional Planning, RUET

**Topics Taught:**
* Review and field-based assessment of urban land-use components, problems, and potentials
* Site identification, reconnaissance surveys, and project methodology formulation
* Field data collection, secondary data organization, and analytical planning techniques
* Preparation of comprehensive land-use plans and site designs
* Execution of group-based planning projects, progress presentations, and report writing
