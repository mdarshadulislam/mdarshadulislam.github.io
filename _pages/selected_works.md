## Undergraduate Thesis

<span id="ug-thesis"></span>

**Exploring Nature-resident Connectedness and Resident Attitudes Towards Tourism: Evidence from a Coastal Destination of Bangladesh**
* **Course:** URP 4000: Project / Thesis
* **Supervisor:** [Dr. Md. Abdul Wakil](https://ruet.ac.bd/mawakilwakil)
* **Publication:** [[DOI: 10.1016/j.jort.2025.100885](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jort.2025.100885)]

* **Description:** Combining the Nature's Contributions to People (NCP) framework and Weber's Theory of Formal and Substantive Rationality (WFSR), this study investigated how residents' connection to nature empowers them through tourism and influences support for local tourism initiatives in Cox's Bazar.
* **Objectives:**
  1. Measure nature connectedness levels among coastal residents.
  2. Analyze the impact of nature connectedness on perceived empowerment from tourism.
* **Key Findings & Implications:** Residents exhibit a strong connection to nature that positively drives support for tourism. However, concerns regarding over-tourism highlight the urgent need for sustainable, nature-aligned planning models that respect community environmental values.
