## Grant Proposals & Applications

**1. Title:** ***Assessment of Human Well-being in Rajshahi City***

* **Status:** Applied / Under Review *(Fiscal Year 2026–2027)*
* **Grant Type:** M.Sc. Research Grant
* **Funding Agency:** University Grants Commission (UGC), Government of Bangladesh
* **Administering Body:** Research and Extension Unit (R&E), Rajshahi University of Engineering & Technology (RUET)
* **Grant Amount Requested:** BDT 3,00,000
* **Role:** Principal Investigator / M.Sc. Scholar

**Objectives:**
1. **Measure Well-being Levels:** Assess and evaluate the level of human well-being among residents across Rajshahi City.
2. **Identify Determinants:** Identify key social, environmental, and personal factors influencing well-being in Rajshahi City to support evidence-based urban planning.
